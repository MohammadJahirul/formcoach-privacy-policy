Privacy policy.

FormCoach is built around local fitness and nutrition records. You can use its core tools without creating an account.

Effective October 11, 2026 · FormCoach for iOS and watchOS

At a glance
  • Your workout, food and wellness records are stored on your device.
  • Camera and nutrition-label processing take place on your device.
  • Optional barcode lookups and packaged-food searches contact Open Food Facts. Apple handles purchases.
  • No advertising, tracking SDKs or third-party analytics are included in the app.

Information stored on your device

FormCoach saves the information you choose to enter: training preferences, routines, workout sets, weights, notes, food diary entries, nutrition targets, recipes, meal plans, grocery selections, body-weight measurements, water intake, daily check-ins and manual activity records. This information is used to provide your journal, planning tools and progress views. We do not operate an account or cloud-sync service for these records.

Camera and selected photos

With camera permission, Motion Lab processes live frames locally to display experimental body landmarks and movement cycles. The barcode scanner also processes camera frames locally. Frames are not saved or uploaded by FormCoach. If you select a nutrition-label photo, Apple Vision recognizes its text on your device. FormCoach does not upload or permanently save that image. Food and nutrition values you confirm can be saved in your local journal.

Optional Apple Health access

If you choose to connect Apple Health, FormCoach asks to read step counts and workouts. Results are displayed in memory and are not copied into FormCoach’s saved files, sent to the developer, or used for advertising. Separately, if you enable workout export and allow writing, FormCoach can save completed strength sessions with start/end times and logged active duration to Apple Health. It does not estimate calories, heart rate or distance. A local list of exported session identifiers helps avoid repeat exports. Turning export off does not delete workouts already saved in Health. You can clear displayed results in the app and manage or revoke access through Apple Health. Lack of available results may mean no records or no read permission.

Apple Watch and Lock Screen

The Watch app keeps its own local workout records. If you enable paired-device sync on iPhone, your next workout plan and completed Watch sessions transfer through Apple’s WatchConnectivity service between your devices. The developer does not receive them. Turning sync off stops future transfers; records already on either device remain. Deleting a record on one device does not delete copies on the other. Optional Live Activities display exercise names, set counts and rest timers on the Lock Screen and supported Dynamic Island, where someone nearby may see them. You can disable this in Connected training.

Barcode searches and food sources

When you request a barcode lookup or explicitly submit a packaged-food search, the barcode or search words are sent over HTTPS to Open Food Facts. That provider receives that query, the app’s identification header, and network information such as your IP address. Your food diary, body weight, Health data and photos are not sent with the request. The provider may handle service logs under its own privacy policy. You can use offline food search or manual entry instead.

The bundled offline catalog uses USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy data and the UK Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset 2021. Offline searches do not contact either publisher. UK data contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Open Food Facts results remain source-labeled and are provided under its applicable database terms.

Purchases and reminders

Apple processes in-app purchases and subscriptions. FormCoach uses StoreKit to verify purchase entitlement on your device; we do not receive your payment-card information. Apple’s handling of account, payment and transaction information is governed by Apple’s policies. Optional daily reminders are scheduled locally after you choose to enable them. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS notification settings.

Sharing, support and this website

Exporting records or sharing a summary opens the system share sheet. The destination you choose controls any copy you send. Exports may contain sensitive information, so review the destination before sharing.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send and use them to respond. Do not include health records unless necessary for your question. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and related obligations; contact us to request deletion.

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub privacy statement. The site has no analytics scripts, advertising or third-party fonts.

Retention, deletion and backups

Local records remain until you delete individual entries, use the relevant reset controls, or remove the app and its data. Training, nutrition and wellness records have separate export and reset controls. Watch records, Apple Health records and notification permissions are managed separately. Exported copies remain wherever you saved or shared them. Device backups may include local records according to your Apple settings; backup retention and restoration are controlled by you and Apple. We cannot recover or erase local files from our support inbox.

Your choices

You can use manual entry without camera or Health permissions, disable reminders, export your records, or erase local data. We do not sell your personal information or use health information for targeted advertising. Contact us about access, correction or deletion of information you sent to support.

Adults and changes to this policy

FormCoach’s nutrition-target setup is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through a FormCoach account because the app has no account system. If a child sends information to support, contact us to request its removal. We will update this page when practices change and revise the effective date.
