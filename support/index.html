When reporting a problem

Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, app version and the steps that led to the issue. Please omit private health records, payment information and passwords.

Restore or manage Pro

Open Today → Pro → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. To manage renewal or cancel, open Apple subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple manages billing and refund requests.

Food scans and portions

Barcode coverage varies by product and country. Always review the package values and portion basis before saving. If a scan cannot be matched or a label cannot be read, use manual entry. Camera features and the experimental Motion Lab need a supported physical device.

Apple Health and reminders

Open Daily habits → Apple Health overview to choose access. Manage permissions in Health → your profile → Apps → FormCoach. Empty results do not necessarily mean zero activity. Daily reminders are optional and can be turned off in Daily reminder settings.

Watch, Lock Screen and Health export

Open Train → Settings → Watch & Lock Screen. Enable paired Watch sync, then send your next workout. The Watch can record a session without the phone nearby and retry delivery when connected. Sync does not mirror deletions between devices. Enable Lock Screen progress separately. Health workout writing requires its own permission; use Export saved sessions to retry a missed export. FormCoach does not estimate workout calories or heart rate. After 30 minutes away, the session pauses when you return and excludes time after you left.

Keep or erase your records

Training, Nutrition and Daily habits settings each provide export and reset controls. Export first if you want a copy. Local data does not sync through a FormCoach account. If the app reports unreadable stored data, export the preserved file before resetting; support cannot retrieve records from your device.

Fitness and nutrition guidance

FormCoach supports general fitness practice and recordkeeping. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe medication, or provide medical treatment. Nutrition calculations and camera features have limitations. For individualized medical or dietary care, consult an appropriately qualified professional.
